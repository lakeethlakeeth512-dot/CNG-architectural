03 / FAQ

Have Questions? We're Here to Help.

Find clear answers about our architectural services, deliverables, software, timelines, and project process.

Start your project ↗
What architectural services do you provide?

We provide 2D floor plans, architectural drawings, house design, 3D modeling, rendering, interior and exterior design, CAD drafting, Revit BIM modeling, and SketchUp 3D modeling.

Can you work with clients internationally?

Yes. We provide remote architectural design, CAD, Revit, and SketchUp services for clients in Sri Lanka and worldwide.

Can I provide my own sketch or idea?

Yes. You can share sketches, reference images, measurements, existing drawings, or design ideas. We can develop your concept into professional 2D drawings and 3D models.

Do you provide revisions?

Yes. Revisions are available according to the selected service package and project requirements. We work with you to refine the design until the agreed requirements are met.

What software do you use?

We use professional design and documentation tools such as AutoCAD, Revit, and SketchUp, along with appropriate rendering and visualization workflows.

What file formats can I receive?

Depending on your project, deliverables can include PDF, DWG, DXF, RVT, SKP, and high-resolution image files. Source files can also be provided when included in the selected package.

Can you create a 3D model from a 2D floor plan?

Yes. We can use your 2D floor plan, elevations, measurements, or reference images to develop a detailed SketchUp or Revit 3D model.

Can you create drawings from an existing building?

Yes. If you provide measurements, sketches, photographs, or existing documentation, we can create or update architectural drawings based on the information provided.

Do you design new houses?

Yes. We can develop modern residential house concepts, floor plans, elevations, 3D models, and architectural visualizations based on your requirements.

Do you provide interior and exterior design?

Yes. We can create interior layouts, exterior concepts, material ideas, furniture arrangements, and 3D visualizations for your project.

Can you convert PDF or image drawings into CAD?

Yes. We can recreate suitable existing PDF, scanned, or image-based drawings as editable CAD drawings, depending on the quality and information available.

Can you provide Revit BIM models?

Yes. We can create Revit-based BIM models and architectural documentation according to the project's required level of detail and available information.

Can you provide SketchUp models?

Yes. We can create detailed SketchUp 3D models for houses, buildings, interiors, exteriors, and architectural concepts.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the project size, complexity, number of drawings, level of detail, and revision requirements. We will provide an estimated delivery time before starting.

What information do you need to start?

Please provide your project requirements, dimensions, sketches, existing drawings, site information, reference images, or any other available project details.

Can you work from simple hand sketches?

Yes. A clear hand sketch can be a useful starting point. We can develop it into organized architectural drawings or a 3D concept.

Can you help with design changes to an existing plan?

Yes. We can modify existing floor plans and develop alternative layouts based on your new requirements.

Do you provide construction-ready drawings?

We can provide detailed architectural drafting and documentation based on the agreed scope. Local building-code compliance, engineering certification, permits, and professional approvals may require a locally licensed architect or engineer.

Do you offer custom packages?

Yes. If your project does not fit a standard package, contact us with your requirements and we can discuss a customized service.

How do I start a project?

Simply contact us with your project requirements, drawings, measurements, or ideas. We'll review the information, discuss the scope, and provide the next steps and quotation.
