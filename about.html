01 / ABOUT CNG

Design that turns ideas into clear solutions.

We are a Sri Lanka-based architectural design studio providing professional design and drafting services for residential and commercial projects. We combine creativity, technical accuracy, and modern technology to turn ideas into clear and practical architectural solutions.

From initial concepts and floor plans to detailed CAD drawings, Revit BIM models, SketchUp 3D models, and realistic visualizations, we help clients bring their projects to life.
