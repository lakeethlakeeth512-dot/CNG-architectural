01 / FROM MEASUREMENTS TO DESIGN

Understand Your Measurements. Build With Confidence.

Whether your measurements are in meters or centimeters, we convert them into the feet-and-inches format commonly used for Indian house plans and architectural drawings.

METERS & CENTIMETERS TO INDIAN FEET & INCHES
1 meter100 cm = 3'-3.37" (approximately 3'-3")
1 centimeter0.3937 inch
FormulaCentimeters ÷ 30.48 = feet
A SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Room size: 4m × 5m

In Indian feet-and-inches format: 13'-1" × 16'-5"
